Tiny ship. Huge trouble.

PiratePlunder is a fast, portrait pirate arcade game for quick voyages and glorious cannon chaos. Pick a ship, grab the wheel and send rival crews to the bottom of the sea.

STEER, LINE UP, FIRE
Drag the big wooden wheel to steer. Hold the left or right cannon button to fire that broadside. Each side reloads separately: line up a rival, unleash a volley, then swing around for the other side. Your ship keeps its heading when you release the wheel.

FOUR SHIPS, FOUR WAYS TO PLUNDER
Swiftfin turns sharply and reloads quickly. Ironclad brings a thick hull and heavy volleys. Redwake lands crushing cannon hits. Golden Gull pulls in more treasure. Every ship earns its own experience and ranks, while your campaign progress is shared across the fleet.

A 50-LEVEL PIRATE ADVENTURE
Follow the Ocean Crown chart through five themed seas: Sunlit Shoals, Mangrove Maze, Ember Archipelago, Frostwake Reach and Tempest Crown. Every mission has a fixed island layout and a finite enemy fleet. Break through channels, flank crossfires, catch convoys and challenge boss captains. Clear every planned encounter to claim victory and earn up to three stars.

BUILD YOUR BROADSIDE
Spend earned gold in the Shipyard to add extra cannons one at a time. Unlock separate port and starboard grapeshot controls with independent reloads. Automated crews and automatic primary fire are top-rank upgrades to work toward, not starting abilities.

AN OCEAN THAT KEEPS GOING
In Endless Ocean, sail through an expanding sea of islands and enemies. Rival ships grow stronger as your voyage continues. Chase a new score, stretch your survival record and see how long your hull can hold together.

QUICK REWARDS, BIG CANNON ENERGY
Collect repairs, shields and cannon powerups. Build a combo, dodge incoming fire and turn a close call into a treasure shower. Daily quests, a fresh Daily Drift challenge and login rewards give you another reason to raise the sails. Unlock 28 achievements as you master the seas.

MADE FOR SHORT VOYAGES
Portrait controls keep the action within reach. Choose your voyage, then pick a ship and sail. A first-voyage tutorial helps you get moving, and local saving keeps your fleet ranks, upgrades, stars and achievements between sessions. Adjust music, sound and reduced motion to suit your play style.

AD-SUPPORTED ADVENTURES
The Android game includes home-screen banner ads and occasional ads between voyages. When an ad is available, choose to watch a rewarded ad after defeat for one full-hull rescue in that voyage. Your score and progress stay intact. Ads require a network connection; the core game remains playable offline.
